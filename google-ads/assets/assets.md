## 4. Call Asset

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Phone number | [Insert Snoots NJ phone number] |
| Call reporting | On |
| Conversion action | Phone call lead (count calls > 60 sec) |
| Ad schedule | Business hours only (avoid after-hours calls that can't be answered) |

## 5. Location Asset

Link your **Google Business Profile** to the account to automatically pull in the Clifton, NJ address.

Steps: Google Ads → Assets → Location → Link Google Business Profile account

Verify the GBP listing is complete:
- Address: [Clifton, NJ clinic address]
- Category: Veterinarian
- Phone: consistent with Call asset
- Hours: accurate and up to date
- 4.9★ rating (carried through automatically)
