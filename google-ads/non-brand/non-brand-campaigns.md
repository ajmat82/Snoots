# Non-Brand Campaigns — Snoots Vet

## Campaign Architecture Overview

| Campaign | Theme | Daily Budget | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| NB — Unlimited Vet Membership | Subscription/membership vet intent | $60/day | 1 |
| NB — Pet Insurance Alternative | Insurance shoppers seeking alternative | $40/day | 2 |
| NB — Local NJ Vet | Geographic near-me intent | $50/day | 3 |
| NB — Pet-Specific | Dog/cat owner intent | $30/day | 4 |

**Total non-brand budget:** ~$180/day to start. Scale winners after 4–6 weeks.

**Bidding strategy (all non-brand):** Start on Maximize Conversions. Once each campaign has 30+ conversions/month, layer in Target CPA. Set initial tCPA around 20–30% above actual CPA to avoid under-delivery while learning.
